The operator requests pushing the complete website to hagency-website and
publishing its pages. Use hagency-org/hagency-website with GitHub Pages at
https://hagency-org.github.io/hagency-website/. Include the completed bilingual
architecture and product rename. Preserve local preview support. All assets,
locale navigation, metadata and interactive links must work under the project
subdirectory. Do not publish credentials or connect demos to live agents.
